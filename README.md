# The Competition File — FL-22

An independent research dossier on the 2026 Florida 22nd Congressional District Republican primary,
built to brief David Burck and his team on the competitive and financial landscape of the race.

Covers:
- Every Republican opponent's biography and primary-night result
- Full FEC campaign finance breakdown for every candidate with disclosed data
- David Burck's complete itemized donor ledger
- A capital-efficiency analysis (vote share won per dollar raised)
- Embedded campaign video and links to David's official channels
- Full sourcing and methodology

Static site, no build step — open `index.html` or serve the folder with any static file server.

Not an official campaign publication.
